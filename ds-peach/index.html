👑 DS Peach Randomizer

New Super Mario Bros. DS texture recolor — hair, dress, gems & face
Recolors work great. New shapes like a ponytail can’t be added here — that needs a model change. The DS packs textures in 4×4 blocks (Tex4x4), so some shades may look slightly blotchy after you inject them with NSMBe.

Paint these native-size PNGs, then re-inject into enemy/peach.nsbmd and map/cursor_peach.nsbmd (same textures). Keep the sizes: peach.png 128×64, peach_face.0.png and peach_face.1.png 32×128.

⬇️ Download

On a phone, ZIP or Share is the easy way to get the three PNGs to your PC (OneDrive, Google Drive, email, or Discord). Or copy the roll link / code, open it on your PC, and download there.

📤 Use your own base PNGs

Optional. Upload stock (or already edited) textures of the exact sizes to recolor from those instead of the built-in originals.

💡 After you download

  • Re-inject the three PNGs into the TEX0 of enemy/peach.nsbmd, then replace both enemy/peach.nsbmd and map/cursor_peach.nsbmd in NSMBe. The map model uses the same textures.
  • If you have the box-side toolkit, inject_nsbmd.py next to this page can patch both NSBMD files for you.
  • Keep a copy of the original .nsbmd so you can undo a bad inject.
